# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-10-23)

First public version. (Primera versión pública.)

- Marks with the keyboard (↑ favorite, ↓ reject, U remove, Ctrl + Z undo) and with the mouse: hover buttons on thumbnails and a mark bar on large photos.
- Mark feedback: a subtle mosaic of checks or crosses behind the photo, and an animated mark icon when marking or arriving at a marked photo (both on by default).
- Viewer with zoom, loupe (Z or middle click), photo-only full view (Space) and full screen (F11).
- Compare with the next photo, the previous one, or both, with synchronized zoom. Esc leaves compare and burst modes.
- Bursts: automatic detection (1, 2 or 3 s between shots), side-by-side view with pages, synchronized zoom, "keep only this one".
- Sharpest photo in each burst and grouping of similar shots (with the on-disk thumbnail cache).
- Thumbnail strip on any side with 1–3 rows or columns, centered or free scrolling, plus a full-screen grid.
- Moment labels, info panel with histogram and clipping warnings, filters, jumps, favorites goal.
- Copy by category (keeping names, dates and EXIF), export and import of lists, favorites slideshow.
- Safe storage: atomic verified saves, daily backups, recovery from damaged files, read-only second window (with a "Solo lectura" badge and retry).
- Dark theme, grouped options menu with icons (Alt), grouped shortcut help (?), adjustable interface size.
- About dialog with author, license and project link; error log in `data\errores.log`.
